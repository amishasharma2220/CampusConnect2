# CampusConnect FAQ

Edit this file to change what the assistant knows about how CampusConnect works.
Keep answers short and only include things that are true.

## Accounts
- Anyone with an @muj.manipal.edu email can sign up at /register. Everyone signs up as a student.
- Forgot your password: password reset isn't available yet. Use the Contact page (/contact).

## Events
- Browse all approved events at /events. Open an event to see details and register.
- Registration is first come, first served until the event's capacity is full.
- Your registered events are under My Events (/student/my-events) and on your dashboard (/student/dashboard).
- Certificates for completed events appear under Certificates (/student/certificates) once the club uploads them.
- Every event is reviewed by a university admin before it appears on the site.

## Clubs
- All active clubs are listed at /clubs.
- To join a club, go to Join a Club (/join-club), pick the club and pay the membership fee through Razorpay.
- The fee is set per club. Your membership is confirmed only after the payment is verified.

## Running a club on CampusConnect
- Club leaders (President, Vice President, General Secretary and similar) can ask to manage their club:
  open your student dashboard and use the "Run a club?" card.
- A university admin reviews the request. If approved, you become the club's admin and get the club dashboard.
- You can have one pending request at a time, and only for a club that doesn't have an admin yet.
- Club admins create events from the club dashboard; each event goes to a university admin for approval.
